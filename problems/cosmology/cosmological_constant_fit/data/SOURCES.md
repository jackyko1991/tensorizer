# Data sources

- **pantheon_plus.csv**: Pantheon+ Type Ia supernova distance moduli, from
  https://github.com/PantheonPlusSH0ES/DataRelease (file Pantheon+SH0ES.dat). Cite Scolnic et al. 2022, ApJ 938, 113
  and Brout et al. 2022, ApJ 938, 110. Licence: publicly released, but no licence is stated for the data files; the
  papers are open access under CC BY 4.0. Processing: z > 0.01, Cepheid calibrators dropped, one row per supernova
  (columns: name, z, mu, sigma_mu).
