# Data sources

Everything here is either derived from CC0 data or computed by us (CC0). No external tool runs at run time or in
the tests; VMTK and OpenFOAM were run once, offline, in throwaway environments outside the project.

## aneurysm_lausanne.stl (default geometry)

- **Source:** Lausanne TOF-MRA Aneurysm Cohort, OpenNeuro ds003949 v1.0.1, subject sub-074, session 20100421
  (doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds003949.v1.0.1). Files used: the N4-bias-corrected, skull-stripped TOF-MRA
  (`derivatives/N4_bias_field_corrected/.../sub-074_ses-20100421_desc-angio_N4bfc_brain_mask.nii.gz`) and the
  aneurysm label (`derivatives/manual_masks/.../sub-074_ses-20100421_desc-Lesion_1_mask.nii.gz`, a sphere around
  the aneurysm, used only to locate it).
- **Licence:** CC0 (dataset_description.json: "License": "CC0").
- **Citation:** Di Noto T. et al., "Towards Automated Brain Aneurysm Detection in TOF-MRA: Open Data, Weak Labels,
  and Anatomical Knowledge", Neuroinformatics (2022), doi:10.1007/s12021-022-09597-0.
- **Processing (ours):** crop of ±14 mm around the label centre; trilinear resampling to 0.3 mm isotropic; Gaussian
  smoothing (σ = 0.4 mm); intensity threshold 215 (a.u.); the connected component through the brightest voxel of the
  label; enclosed cavities filled. Then, offline with vmtk 1.5.0 / VTK 9.2.6: VTK marching cubes on the Gaussian-
  smoothed mask, box clip 0.5 mm inside the crop (open ends), largest component, vmtksurfacesmoothing (Taubin, 30
  iterations, pass band 0.1), vmtksurfaceremeshing (edge 0.55 mm), vmtkcenterlines (profileidlist; inlet = largest
  profile), vmtkflowextensions (-adaptivelength 1 -extensionratio 5), vmtksurfacecapper (centre point). Units mm, in a
  local frame of the crop (no scanner coordinates, nothing identifying). Binary STL; the 2-byte attribute of each
  triangle is 0 wall, 1 inlet cap, 2, 3, 4 outlet caps (largest first).
- **Caveat:** a threshold segmentation of TOF-MRA is approximate: slow flow in the dome and thin branches lose
  signal, so the lumen near small vessels is uncertain and branches thinner than about 0.6 mm in radius are missing.

## aneurysm.stl (synthetic geometry)

Generated by us (CC0); see aneurysm.stl.source.

## vmtk_reference_centerlines.csv, vmtk_reference_centerlines_lausanne.csv

VMTK 1.5.0 centrelines of the two surfaces (computed by us, CC0; for the Lausanne one, derived from CC0 data).
Headers give the commands.

## openfoam_reference_womersley.csv, openfoam_reference_aneurysm_slice.csv, openfoam_reference_aneurysm_wss.csv

OpenFOAM v1912 pimpleFoam runs (computed by us, CC0): a Womersley pipe, and the Lausanne aneurysm on our own voxel
mesh exported as a polyMesh. Headers give the solver settings.

## inflow_waveform.csv

Analytic ICA-like waveform made by us (CC0), with the mean flow and peak/mean ratio of Ford et al. (2005),
doi:10.1088/0967-3334/26/4/013.
