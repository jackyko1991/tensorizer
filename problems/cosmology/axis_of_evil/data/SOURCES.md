# Data sources

- **wmap_ilc9_nside16.csv**: WMAP 9-year Internal Linear Combination (ILC) temperature map, from NASA's LAMBDA
  archive (https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov). Licence: NASA data, public domain. Cite Bennett et al. 2013, ApJS 208, 20.
  Processing: degraded here from HEALPix N_side = 512 to 16 (columns: galactic longitude, latitude in degrees, T in μK).
